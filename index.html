Good to know

Questions brides ask me

The things worth knowing before you write — answered here, so you do not have to ask.

Do you come to me on the wedding day?+

Yes — always. I come to you at home, at your hotel or at the venue, so you never have to travel on your own morning. Travel is charged per visit, for the trial and for the wedding day: €35 within Amsterdam, €60 up to 25 km, €95 up to 50 km. Further afield on request, and parking at cost where it applies.

Is a trial session included in the price?+

Yes, every bridal booking is a trial and the wedding day together, and the price is simply split between the two. At the trial we take our time and find your look — soft and natural, classic and radiant, or full glam. On the day itself I recreate it, so there are no surprises in the mirror.

How long will it take on the wedding day?+

That is exactly what your trial is for. Together we decide what your look will be, and only then do I know how much time it needs — a soft natural look and a fully pinned updo are simply not the same amount of work. So I will not put a number here that I could not keep. What I do promise is this: I plan your timing generously, so that nothing is rushed. On the one day that ought to feel unhurried, you should be sitting calmly — not watching the clock.

How far in advance should I book?+

I am currently taking bookings for 2026 and 2027 weddings. Summer Saturdays go first, so as soon as you have your date is not too early. Trial sessions are by appointment and we arrange yours once your date is held.

Can you style my bridesmaids and my mother as well?+

Gladly — beside you on the day, so that everyone is ready together and nobody is left waiting. €85 per person for makeup, €85 for hair, or €150 for both. Just tell me how many of you there will be.

What if my day starts before sunrise?+

That happens more often than you would think, and it is no trouble at all. A start before 7:00 is charged at €45 and before 6:00 at €85. On Sundays a surcharge of 25% applies and on Dutch public holidays 50%.

What is included in the price?+

Products, skin preparation, lashes and your touch-up kit for the day. Everything is named openly in the price list, so nothing appears afterwards that we have not talked about.

How do I secure my date?+

A 50% deposit at your trial session holds your date; the remaining 50% is due on the wedding day. Until the trial, nothing is owed.

Which languages do you speak?+

German and English. I trained in Germany and I work in Amsterdam, so we can plan your day in whichever of the two feels more natural to you. Many of the brides I work with here are international, and English is our everyday language.

Is VAT added on top?+

No. All prices are final — what you see is what you pay.
