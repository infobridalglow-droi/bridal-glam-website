About Anja

Passion, Heart & Expertise

My professional makeup artistry transforms bridal beauty into something truly luminous — giving you that signature radiant glow on the most important day of your life.

My journey began in 2014, when I graduated as a certified Makeup Artist and Hairstylist from a renowned academy in Germany — turning a lifelong passion into my profession.

To me, makeup is so much more than just foundation, eyeshadow, or the perfect lip color. It is the art of bringing your inner light to the surface — so every photo, every glance, every moment glows.
