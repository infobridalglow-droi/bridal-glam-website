How it goes

Your trial

The trial is where we get to know each other. I listen to what you have in mind, and I look at whether the makeup you wish for suits your face. Then we fine-tune it together.

I explain each step as I go, so you always know where we are, and we trade ideas along the way. As a professional makeup artist and hairstylist I am at your side with advice whenever you want it, so you can make the best choice for your day. At the end we look at the whole picture, makeup and hair, and ask one question: do you love it?

Your wedding morning with me

I arrive, say hello, and set up my station so that everything is ready before we start. We go through your makeup and your hair once more, so the morning runs exactly to your plan, and I keep an eye on the time, so you don’t have to.

Then we begin with skincare. Well-prepared skin is why foundation melts in instead of sitting on top, and why everything lasts all day. From there we work from the eyes outwards. If you have booked hair as well, we usually start with the hair.

At the end we look at everything together. You check how you feel in your makeup, and we adjust until it is exactly yours. A setting spray locks it in, and your touch-up kit is ready for the day. If you like, I help you into your dress and with the veil. Then I say thank you, and wish you a wonderful day.
