How I work

What you can count on

When you recommend me, your name is on the line too. This is what your couples get.

I come to the bride

Home, hotel or venue, anywhere in and around Amsterdam. No studio visits on the wedding morning, no extra logistics for you.

English & German

Certified makeup artist and hairstylist, trained in Germany in 2014. Many of my brides are international couples; the trial and the morning run in English or German.

Calm on the morning

A bride wrote that I noticed straight away how nervous she was, and that with a calm, warm way I managed to settle her. That is the job: the look, and the mood in the room.

Made for the camera, made to last

Professional techniques and premium products: a glow that photographs softly in daylight and holds from the first "I do" to the last dance. Bridesmaids, mothers and guests are welcome in the same morning.
